## Introduction, motivation and research questions

The operational task is a daily forecast of the next 24 hourly values of station-level **estimated charging energy**. A target with only 7, 14 or 28 days of initially permitted history may not support reliable estimation from scratch. Source pretraining offers an initialization, but keeping the representation close to the Source encoder can either help or hinder adaptation as target observations accumulate. The stations are historical simulations of limited-history targets; the data do not establish that they were newly opened physical stations. The forecast and target definitions follow [Report 1 §§1–4](../../adaptive_retention_report_1.pdf) and the [input acceptance contract](../data/INPUT_ACCEPTANCE.md).

Report 1 frames a **candidate**, not a proven novelty claim: source forecast error, source prediction dispersion and observed load drift are proxies for the value of retaining Source knowledge. None directly measures the difference between learning processes with different retention strengths. The proposed controller therefore compares those processes over a completed block and switches only when a lower confidence bound on observed paired improvement exceeds a locked threshold. This idea overlaps existing online selection and adaptive regularization; its novelty and practical gain require comparison rather than assertion.

The three research questions are: **RQ1**, how retention affects forecast error and when proxy signals fail to track its benefit; **RQ2**, whether evidence-based switching improves on fixed retention, simple online best-λ and proxy controllers; and **RQ3**, whether outcomes are stable across stations, target-history budgets and contexts at acceptable computational cost. The design logic is Literature → Existing methods → limitations as analyzed by this team → candidate gap → RQs → method requirements → proposed method → experiments. The [Master Research Flow](../MASTER_RESEARCH_FLOW.md) explains this logic and distinguishes conceptual design from executed evidence.

## Problem formulation, data and split protocol

The task predicts the next 24 hourly station-energy values from 24 observed hours at daily origins, using permitted future calendar features. Source training precedes target initialization at 7, 14 or 28 days. Forecasts are issued before next-day labels are read. Only complete, valid 24-hour horizons are scored or used for updates. Report 1, the [input contract](../data/INPUT_ACCEPTANCE.md) and [method report](hung-39-method-implementation.md) define the causal boundaries. The executed Development model has 41,696 parameters. Controller decisions use a five-complete-day minimum, a 14-day block and day-block bootstrap lower bound. The Hùng/Quyền head and bootstrap convention is still under scientific review.

The accepted target is derived/estimated kWh, not a directly audited electricity-meter reading. `X_hist` has shape `[batch,24,7]`: scaled recent demand, calendar and observed-history mask; `C_future` is `[batch,24,5]` and contains only known future calendar; `Y` and the forecast are `[batch,24]`. A genuine observed zero remains distinct from a missing observation. The input pipeline uses pre-deployment Source examples and disjoint Source/Development/Final entity roles; exact eligibility and hashes are in the [input handoff](input-handoff.md) and [data integration report](data-integration.md). UrbanEV station-level SZH is the primary executed cohort. CHARGED site-level six-city evidence is supplementary because execution lineage, site semantics and SZH geographic/temporal independence have not been accepted.

At each daily origin, the runner stores every candidate forecast before reading any arriving label. A forecast with no usable history is logged as skipped; a forecast with an incomplete 24-hour label vector remains issued but unscored and cannot trigger an update. The exclusive horizon-end availability rule is a published-data replay assumption, not measured real-world label-arrival latency. Upstream processing, timezone/bin choices, DST and Shenzhen overlap limit strict online and external-validity claims. The three target-history budgets are separate experiments, and Final labels cannot select λ, thresholds, features, calibration or analysis groups after the lock.

## Executed GRU, retention and controller method

The executed Hùng [`ForecastGRU`](../../experiments/hung_sprint2/model.py) has a two-layer GRU encoder with hidden dimension 64. A `120→8` ReLU projection of the 24×5 known future-calendar tensor is concatenated with the last latent vector; a `72→24` linear layer and Softplus produce nonnegative forecasts. The [historical parameter audit](hung-39-method-implementation.md) counted 41,696 trainable parameters. The head topology is an **executed identity**; unresolved Hùng–Quyền parity is recorded below, and no frozen Final weights were modified to force a match.

For complete labels on the normalized target scale, the training objective follows Report 1 §5 and the executed [training implementation](../../experiments/hung_sprint2/training.py):

\[
L_{\mathrm{forecast}}=\frac{1}{24B}\sum_{b=1}^{B}\sum_{h=1}^{24}|\hat y_{b,h}-y_{b,h}|,\qquad
L_{\mathrm{retain}}=\frac{1}{64B}\sum_{b=1}^{B}\sum_{k=1}^{64}\left(\frac{z_{T,b,k}-z_{S,b,k}}{s_k+10^{-6}}\right)^2,\qquad
L_{\mathrm{total}}=L_{\mathrm{forecast}}+\lambda L_{\mathrm{retain}}.
\]

The Source encoder reference sees the same target-normalized input but is frozen in evaluation mode. Each `s_k` is the Source-training population latent standard deviation, held fixed. λ=0 is Source-initialized full fine-tuning; Target-only starts without Source weights. Source training in the historical #39 representative run used one pass over 65,888 permitted examples, Adam learning rate 0.001 and batch 32; target initialization used one pass, batch 2 and a separate optimizer. Those settings are run identities, not evidence they are optimal.

At a block boundary, the [`runner`](../../experiments/hung_sprint2/runner.py) forks the same active model and optimizer into candidates at λ∈{0,0.1,0.2}. Their data order, update schedule, normalization and random streams are paired. For valid day `t`, `Δ_{t,j}=MAE_{active,t}−MAE_{j,t}` on the same 24 arrived labels. The controller requires at least five valid days in a 14-day block. It uses 100 paired day-block bootstrap resamples of segment length two, a one-sided α/2=0.025 lower bound per challenger (α=0.05), and δ=0. If no challenger has `LCB>δ`, it carries the **trained** active model/optimizer forward. Otherwise it selects highest LCB, then nearest current λ, then fixed configured order. The bootstrap addresses a decision within a block; neither the 100 draws nor Bonferroni across two challengers establishes long-run error control under repeated blocks. The [event journal/checkpoint contract](hung-39-method-implementation.md) preserves cursor, pending labels, model/optimizer and RNG state for eligible new runs; historical model-stage checkpoints do not retrospectively gain RNG snapshots.

## Comparators, ablations and analysis protocol

The required comparator set from Report 1 §8.1 is Seasonal Naive with fallback, Target-only GRU, Frozen Source, Source-initialized full fine-tuning with λ=0, fixed retention, simple online best-λ, and source-error, uncertainty and drift controllers. Simple online best-λ shares candidate/block/checkpoint/update budgets and differs in selection by mean error without the confidence test. The three proxy controllers use only decision-time information; a custom proxy is not a paper reproduction. Report 1 §8.3 requires five ablations: without confidence, fixed λ, parameter instead of latent retention, changed block size and no Source initialization. Identical runs reused between baseline and ablation labels are counted once as physical evidence. [Variant identities and costs](hung-final-analysis-20261029-v1/variant_events.csv) allow the reader to separate observed variants from conceptual requirements.

Primary MAE and supplementary RMSE use original kWh across all scored hours per job; WAPE is the scored absolute-error sum divided by the positive scored-label sum **within each job**. Paired Transfer Gain is `(MAE_Target-only−MAE_method)/MAE_Target-only` for a common station/budget/seed and identical scored-day label identities. Negative Transfer Rate is the fraction of predeclared station units with negative station-first gain. A zero denominator yields a separately reported absolute gain, not an invented ratio. The frozen aggregation is day → station/seed → station → budget; equal physical-seed means precede equal-station means. The descriptive exact station bootstrap retains station as the unit but only three Final stations, so uncertainty about time dependence and population generalization remains. Controller switches, old-policy audit, three candidate updates/inferences, Source/Target training and provider overhead are reported separately where observed; unmeasured fields remain null.

### Answers to the three research questions within measured scope

- **RQ1 — retention and proxy alignment:** Main beats Target-only on paired MAE in the three locked SZH stations at all three budgets, so Source initialization plus its executed adaptation process has a positive observed transfer gain **relative to that comparator**. Main's MAE and WAPE are nevertheless slightly worse than Source-error, full fine-tuning and online best-λ on the same balanced Final subset. These comparisons do not isolate a causal effect of λ alone or establish when each proxy fails across a wider population. [Station-first comparisons](hung-final-analysis-20261029-v1/station_first.csv) retain every method and interval; [context/proxy Development audit](hung-development-context-proxy-20261029-v2/report.md) gives measured conditional slices with their denominators.
- **RQ2 — confidence versus comparators:** The proposed controller is **not empirically best** in this bounded Final. Fixed retention MAE is 7.961/7.952/7.860 kWh and online best-λ is 7.982/7.949/7.884 kWh across 7/14/28 days, each lower than Main's 8.051/8.042/7.916 kWh. These are station-first equal-seed means in a balanced three-station cohort; differences are small and descriptive. This evidence does not support an RQ2 superiority claim. The recorded 313 switches among 1,863 controller decisions are actions, not attribution of benefit or harm.
- **RQ3 — stability and cost:** The bounded Final has 3 stations, 3 physical seeds for stochastic variants and 3 budgets; Seasonal Naive has only one physical seed. Main's positive Target-only gain holds across the three stations per budget, but the exact station bootstrap has only three independent station units. The 360 replay-stage durations total 4,329.8 s, whereas 54 upstream stage-duration fields are null and provider overhead is substantial. Memory and complete end-to-end cost are not measured comparably for every method. Stability across other UrbanEV stations, CHARGED cities, temporal regimes and implementation variants remains unknown.

These answers distinguish observed associations from the original hypotheses. They do not use the held-out Final scores to alter the frozen λ grid, controller or selected cohort.

## Limitations, conclusion and reproducibility handoff

Development supports conditional findings on scorable stations in a frozen input with substantial structured label missingness. The held-out 360-job Final supports a positive Main-versus-Target-only paired MAE gain on three SZH stations, while several alternative methods have slightly lower observed MAE than Main. The historical Main receipt class is weaker; Kaggle saved-version bindings and Colab Drive bytes still need peer authentication, and Block7 comparability, model/controller parity and six-city CHARGED lineage remain open. The scientific conclusion is bounded: technical Final execution and measured subset effects are reproducible, but broad E2E generalization and full Hùng research acceptance are not established.

## Threats to validity and unresolved acceptance

**Measurement and data validity.** The target is estimated energy. Scored labels are conditional on complete 24-hour horizons; Development missingness is structured rather than random. The historical replay's assumed label arrival and upstream time/mask processing have not been established as real-time availability. UrbanEV/SZH and CHARGED/SZH may overlap geographically, and CHARGED's sites are not automatically the same unit as UrbanEV stations.

**Internal validity.** Frozen Final code and inputs protect against post-result retuning of the 360-job subset, but Block7's historical Source/Target payload comparability and the trained Hùng–Quyền head/optimizer/runner parity remain unresolved. The old-policy audit is retrospective and cannot prove a switch was wrong given information available at decision time. Repeated sequential confidence checks do not inherit a global type-I error guarantee from a per-block Bonferroni adjustment.

**External and statistical validity.** The Final contains only three SZH UrbanEV stations. Station-first intervals with `n=3` are descriptive; temporal dependence is not fully represented by treating dates as independent station replications. The locked Final excludes 370 other eligible UrbanEV IDs and all six CHARGED city scopes. No full-population or cross-city effectiveness claim is justified. CHARGED results remain `SUPPLEMENTARY_ONLY` pending execution lineage and scope review. The Colab #39 technical 63/63-stage proof lacks non-author Drive-byte authentication.

**Cost and reproducibility.** Three trained candidates, a separate audit branch and provider I/O all contribute to cost. Saved stage timings omit 54 fields, so a complete cost ratio versus a one-model baseline is not available. GitHub Actions on the preceding `f76204ce56e90521d987ea84fe2dd6ebab7d6abc` checkpoint was blocked before tests started by account payment/spending limit: `CI_NOT_EXECUTED — ACCOUNT_BILLING_OR_SPENDING_LIMIT`; its clean local suite passed 500 tests with one skip. CI for this draft's eventual head must be checked separately in PR #72. Software checks do not substitute for scientific review.

## Conclusion and future work

The executed study demonstrates a reproducible, frozen **bounded** 360-job UrbanEV Final with independent remote archive readback and checkpoint-recomputed MAE/RMSE/WAPE. Main transfers positively relative to Target-only on the locked three-station subset, while several alternatives achieve slightly lower observed errors. The evidence therefore supports a constrained empirical finding and a functioning evaluation protocol, **not** a general superiority or novelty claim. Completing the original E2E scope requires reviewer decisions on Block7, architecture/controller parity, CHARGED lineage and Colab #39, plus a reviewed plan for the excluded cohort/city scope. Future work should examine broader station and geographic coverage, stronger dependence-aware uncertainty and complete measured cost under an approved new experiment identity; none may retroactively change this Final lock.
