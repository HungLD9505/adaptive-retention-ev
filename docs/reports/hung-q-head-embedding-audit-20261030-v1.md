## Exact functional construction

Hùng's trained head computes `Softplus(W_z z + W_c ReLU(A c + a) + b)`, with 64 latent inputs, 120 flattened calendar inputs and eight calendar hidden units. Qyroven's 64-unit head computes `Softplus(V ReLU(U[z;c]+u)+v)`. The identity `ReLU(t) − ReLU(−t) = t` allows each of the 24 scalar rows of `W_z z` to use two Qyroven hidden units (48 total). Eight more units copy `ReLU(A c+a)`, and the remaining eight units stay zero. The output layer copies the corresponding signed latent and calendar coefficients and Hùng's bias. This embeds **the same initial forecast function** inside Qyroven's declared topology without claiming that their trainable parameters or optimizer states are isomorphic.
