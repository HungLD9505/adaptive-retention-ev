## Written method versus executed choices

The method authority is [Report 1](../../adaptive_retention_report_1.pdf), especially §§5–7 (PDF pages 3–5). The [Master Research Flow](../MASTER_RESEARCH_FLOW.md) §§model/controller and [Sprint Plan](../SPRINT_PLAN.md) §2 restate its constraints; the Sprint Plan explicitly requires the positive λ, bootstrap and challenger settings to be locked on Development. These documents fix: two GRU layers with hidden size 64; a 24-hour historical input and 24 nonnegative outputs; last GRU representation combined with *future calendar* in an MLP and Softplus head; normalized 24-hour mean MAE plus λ times scaled latent retention against a frozen evaluation-mode Source encoder; matched three-candidate model/optimizer/data/order/budget/update schedule; forecasts stored before labels; complete-day paired MAE, one-sided day-block-bootstrap lower bound, Bmin and δ; and carry-forward of the **trained** selected candidate, including when keeping the active level.

The written wording says to concatenate the final representation with future calendar and then pass it through an MLP and Softplus. It **does not** give MLP depth, width, calendar encoding width, tensor layout or exact parameter shapes. The executed Hùng head embeds flattened future calendar `120→8` with Linear/ReLU *before* concatenation, then applies `Linear(72,24)` and Softplus; total model parameters: 41,696. The byte-pinned Qyroven head concatenates the raw 120 calendar values with the 64-dimensional latent, then applies `Linear(184,64)`/ReLU/`Linear(64,24)`/Softplus; total: 52,376. Qyroven follows the literal operation order more directly. Whether Hùng's factorization satisfies the intended “MLP combines representation with future calendar” is **an interpretation for a non-author reviewer**, not a defect established by the text alone. Their full head weights have no direct same-shape checkpoint correspondence.

Report 1 fixes day-level paired improvement and one-sided block-bootstrap LCB. Its phrase allowing `α/2` for two challengers is permissive; the Sprint Plan requires the treatment to be Development-locked. Both executed controllers use `α/2`, moving noncircular blocks over the ordered **valid-day sequence**, and shared resampled positions across challengers. Report 1 does **not** fix RNG stream derivation, quantile interpolation versus order statistic, or whether a gap between valid days breaks an eligible block. Hùng uses `SHA256(seed:block_id)` and an order statistic at `ceil(αR)-1`; Qyroven hashes a structured stream identity and linearly interpolates the quantile. Qyroven also enforces segment length `< Bmin`, while Hùng permits equality. These are reproducibility and fairness choices, not interchangeable implementations of one byte-identical decision rule. The [varying-day fixture](hung-q-parity-20261027-v1.md#varying-day-decision-discrepancy) reverses one marginal switch; stream derivation alone is sufficient for that fixture, and quantile rules differ too. The written contract does not settle which convention is scientifically preferable.

## Exact trained-state trace required after the decision

Use one accepted **Development** station/cohort, origin schedule, 7/14/28-day prefix budget and seed, with identical input manifest, scaler, Source checkpoint and latent-scale vector. Give both runners the same *reviewed* head topology and tensor-mapped Source/Target weights, optimizer slots, RNG streams, λ levels, controller constants, update schedule and missing/late-label fixture; record SHA-256 of each initial serialized state. Compare, origin by origin:

1. Available history/calendar/mask and each label-access time; all three saved 24-hour forecasts **before** label availability.
2. After a complete label: candidate daily normalized losses, 64-dimensional target and frozen Source latents, forecast and retention losses, model gradients, optimizer step counters and state hashes; verify no Source gradient and no update for incomplete labels.
3. At each block boundary: valid-day membership and order, paired improvements, bootstrap draw identities/indices or stream hashes, per-challenger LCBs, eligibility/ties, chosen λ, audit fork, and model/optimizer/RNG hashes carried into the next block, including a keep decision.
4. At a checkpoint boundary: compare uninterrupted run with split save/resume for forecast, decision and state equivalence; document deterministic tolerance (including dtype/device) **before** examining differences. Preserve both raw and normalized event traces so normalization cannot hide update-count or state divergence.
