## 1. Task, method and training protocol

The task is a daily forecast of the next 24 hourly station-energy values from the preceding 24-hour history, known future calendar and observed/missing mask. UrbanEV station energy is estimated kWh, not a direct-meter claim. The source cohort is pre-deployment; target initialization uses only the allowed 7-, 14- or 28-day Development prefix. Forecasts are stored before labels; a day is scored and used for online updates only after all 24 valid labels are available. Final labels have not been used to choose a model, threshold, seed, cohort or calibration. See the [input contract](../data/INPUT_ACCEPTANCE.md) and [method implementation](hung-39-method-implementation.md).

Hung's initial backbone is a two-layer GRU with hidden size 64 and a calendar MLP/Softplus 24-output head (41,696 parameters). Source training uses 65,888 eligible examples from 871 source station IDs per pass, one pass, batch 32, Adam learning rate 0.001 (2,059 updates per Source stage). The Target starts from Source weights, uses a separate Adam state and one initial pass at batch 2. The frozen evaluation-mode Source encoder supplies the latent reference; forecast MAE on normalized 24-hour targets is combined with source-standard-deviation-scaled squared latent distance at weight `lambda`. `lambda=0` is Source-initialized full fine-tuning, not Target-only training. These are executed diagnostic settings, not a proof that the Source pass or hyperparameters are optimal.

The prequential runner forks the same active model/optimizer into three paired candidates with `lambda={0,0.1,0.2}` at 14-day blocks. It records every candidate forecast before labels, then uses arrived, complete-label paired daily errors. The confidence decision requires at least five days and a one-sided two-challenger day-block-bootstrap lower bound above zero; the recorded diagnostic implementation uses block length 2 and 100 resamples. The selected trained model and optimizer continue, including when the current level is kept. An isolated old-policy audit does not influence the decision. Checkpoint/resume preserves issued forecasts and runner RNG. [Method details](hung-39-method-implementation.md) distinguish the proposal from the exact executed configuration.

The matched comparison plan declares **nine baseline families and five ablation categories** across 69 station/budget/seed cells, budgets 7/14/28 days and seeds 7/11/19. They include Target-only, Frozen Source, full fine-tuning, fixed retention, Online Best-lambda, three proxy controllers and Seasonal Naive; ablations include confidence removal, fixed lambda, parameter retention, block-size changes and no Source initialization. Deterministic aliases and identical configurations are reused rather than counted as new physical runs. Block-7 historical runs are kept unmatched until the fairness review. The immutable 782-job allocation is [recorded here](evidence/hung-development-allocation-20261027-v1.json): pilot 6 + local 216 + Kaggle 560. Historical Main 69 and Block-7 69 are separate from the 782 new jobs. Only **36/69 cells** had a numeric MAE/RMSE; **33 were null**, not zero error. It recorded 32 switches and 382 keeps across 414 block decisions. The audit rechecked 354/354 checkpoint/journal hashes, 20,394 forecast tensors and 18,313 events, and recomputed every cell's stored metric to within `3.55e-15` kWh. These are different entities/cohorts; they cannot rank history budgets or show a method advantage. [All 69 rows and exact provenance](hung-39-representative-local-20261025-evidence.json) are retained.
