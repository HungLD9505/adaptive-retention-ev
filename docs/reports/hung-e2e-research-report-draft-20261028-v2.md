## Research task and method

The task forecasts the next 24 hourly station-energy values from the previous 24 hours at each daily origin, with permitted future calendar inputs. Source training uses the pre-deployment partition. Target adaptation uses the assigned 7, 14 or 28 days of initial target history; subsequent forecasts are issued before labels arrive, and scoring/updating requires all 24 valid labels. Missing labels are never filled or counted as zero. [Input acceptance](../data/INPUT_ACCEPTANCE.md) and the [executed method report](hung-39-method-implementation.md) give the causal and implementation contracts.

Hùng's executed two-layer hidden-64 GRU has a 24-output calendar/Softplus head (41,696 parameters). Its Target objective combines forecast MAE and lambda-weighted latent retention against a frozen Source encoder. The controller evaluates paired candidates at lambda 0, 0.1 and 0.2 and uses arrived paired errors, a five-complete-day minimum and a day-block-bootstrap lower-bound rule at 14-day blocks. The executed head and bootstrap differ from Qyroven's implementation; a [peer contract and common trace](hung-scientific-gates-review-dossier-20261028-v2.md) are still required. These executed settings are not automatically the Final settings.

## Development results

The [reproducible CPU audit](hung-development-audit-20261028-v2/report.md) pins the 851-run source and 782-job ledger SHA-256 values. **444 runs have scored metrics and 407 runs have zero scored days.** All zero-scored runs created forecasts, but every created forecast remained unscored. They occupy 11 station × budget cells (three at 7 days, four at 14 days, four at 28 days), each with 37 method/seed runs. The saved artifacts do not establish whether the missing labels could arrive later. The exact IDs and per-run counts are in [zero_scored.csv](hung-development-audit-20261028-v2/zero_scored.csv).

There are 816 valid paired cells and 748 blocked paired cells. Every blocked row records `no_paired_scored_days`; the [blocked-pair table](hung-development-audit-20261028-v2/blocked_pairs.csv) preserves its method, reference, seed and station. The [72 paired groups](hung-development-audit-20261028-v2/paired_uncertainty.csv) report original-kWh MAE/RMSE gain, exact denominators, descriptive station-bootstrap 95% intervals and negative-transfer counts where supplied. Gains compare identical scored-day hashes within station/seed, then weight seeds equally within station and stations equally within budget. A positive gain means lower method error. Only 3–5 stations per budget are scorable; intervals are descriptive, not population guarantees. No global method ranking or Final selection follows from rows with different reference/cohort intersections.

Against Target-only, the audit records zero negative-transfer stations for ten eligible methods at each budget (0/3, 0/4 and 0/5 respectively); `no_source` has 3/3, 3/4 and 3/5 adverse stations. Seasonal-naive NTR is null in the merged source, not zero. The exact method names, gains and denominators are in the paired table. This is a Development observation on the scorable subset, not proof of generalization or controller superiority.

The [Kaggle decision trace table](hung-development-audit-20261028-v2/switch_audit_kaggle.csv) binds 560 rows to ledger archive/receipt/manifest hashes. Among 413 rows with saved decision lists, confidence has 1,002 decisions/91 observed switches and Online Best-lambda has 408/90; 147 fixed-policy rows have no decision list and retain null decision/switch counts. The other 291 local/historical run manifests are outside this repository and were not used for a complete switching claim. The [cost strata](hung-development-audit-20261028-v2/cost_by_method_device.csv) cover final-stage duration for 851 runs; 690 have a candidate/audit split, while 161 legacy rows retain that split as null. These times exclude upstream training, upload and readback, and devices are not paired for a hardware-speed comparison.

## Interpretation and limitations

The saved Development results support technical execution, reproducible accounting and conditional paired comparisons. They do not establish that the controller is superior across the full planned cohort: 407 runs have no scored day, 748 pair cells are blocked, Block7 comparability remains undecided, and the Hùng/Qyroven head/bootstrap/runner contracts are not aligned. CHARGED attribution and independent review remain open. A favorable Development MAE cannot decide the protocol or select a Final configuration after Final labels are opened.

RQ1–RQ3 therefore have bounded Development answers only. The results show how observed scorable stations behave under the recorded methods and null/missingness rules; they do not prove effectiveness on missing cells, an independent CHARGED geography, or a held-out Final cohort. The older [draft v1](hung-e2e-research-report-draft-20261028.md) is preserved as a historical snapshot and its 371-run/222-proof statements are superseded by this report and the v16 register.
