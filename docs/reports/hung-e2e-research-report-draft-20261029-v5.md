## Research question and executed protocol

The task predicts the next 24 hourly station-energy values from 24 observed history hours at each daily origin, using permitted future calendar features. Source training precedes target deployment; target initialization uses the assigned 7, 14 or 28 days. Forecasts are issued before next-day labels are read. Only a complete, valid 24-hour label horizon can be scored or used to update. Missing values are not imputed or scored as zero. The [input contract](../data/INPUT_ACCEPTANCE.md) and [method report](hung-39-method-implementation.md) describe the executed causal boundaries.

Hùng's executed two-layer hidden-64 GRU has a 24-output calendar/Softplus head (41,696 parameters). Target adaptation combines forecast MAE and lambda-weighted latent retention against a frozen Source encoder. The controller compares paired lambda candidates 0, 0.1 and 0.2 using arrived errors, a five-complete-day minimum, a 14-day block and a day-block-bootstrap lower-bound rule. These are executed Development settings, not an approved Hùng Final lock. The Hùng/Qyroven head and bootstrap contract remains under independent review.

## Results from saved Development evidence

The [CPU audit](hung-development-audit-20261028-v2/report.md) pins the 851-run merged source and the 782-job ledger SHA. It reports 444 scorable runs and **407 zero-scored runs**. These 407 runs occupy 11 station × budget cells, each with 37 method/seed records. The new [frozen-input label audit](hung-zero-score-label-audit-20261028-v1/report.md) binds all 407 to the original Development input ZIP and checks every one of their 550 unique created origins. Every origin has at least one invalid/missing hourly label within the 24-hour horizon; 3,970 bins have `file_valid=false` and `energy_kwh=null`. No created origin has a complete horizon that merely arrives after replay. The 550 origins repeated across the 37 runs per cell account for **20,350 unscored forecasts**. This establishes incomplete labels in the pinned input snapshot; it does not establish the upstream raw-data cause or authorize retroactive rescoring. Nine cells had no initial target trainable examples and used Source-checkpoint-only initialization; the two station-1987 cells had 1 and 13 initial examples. Initialization and replay scoring are distinct.

The matrix contains 816 valid paired cells and **748 blocked pairs**, each with saved reason `no_paired_scored_days`. The [72 paired groups](hung-development-audit-20261028-v2/paired_uncertainty.csv) preserve original-kWh MAE/RMSE gain, exact paired-day and station/seed denominators, descriptive station-bootstrap intervals, and negative-transfer counts where supplied. Gains use identical scored-day hashes within station/seed; seed and station contributions are then weighted equally within budget. Only 3–5 stations per budget are scorable, so intervals are descriptive and rows with different cohort intersections cannot be pooled into a global ranking.

Against Target-only, ten eligible methods have zero negative-transfer stations at each budget (0/3, 0/4, 0/5); `no_source` has 3/3, 3/4, 3/5 adverse stations. Seasonal-naive NTR is null in the merged source, not zero. These are conditional Development observations on scorable stations and cannot establish held-out generalization or controller superiority. The complete method-level gains, RMSE, intervals and denominators remain in the paired table.

The [full switch-manifest audit](hung-development-switch-audit-20261028-v4/report.md) binds **851/851** physical run manifests to their saved source. Of these, 690 carry decision lists with **3,933 decisions and 386 observed level changes**; 161 fixed-policy runs have only issued-count evidence and retain null decision/switch fields. Local new jobs have ledger archive/receipt/manifest SHA bindings; the 69 historical Main manifests are bound through the review capsule's internal ledger but have no individual job receipts. The 560 Kaggle rows retain the prior v2 proof. Switching is a saved action count, not an effectiveness result. The [cost table](hung-development-audit-20261028-v2/cost_by_method_device.csv) covers final-stage duration for all 851 runs, with candidate/audit split available for 690 and null for 161 legacy rows. A [fresh 161-run archive audit](hung-development-legacy-cost-recovery-20261029.md) checked every legacy manifest, receipt, log, journal and checkpoint without finding an authenticated candidate/audit partition. Those 161 splits cannot be recovered from the saved bytes and remain null. The duration table excludes upstream training, upload and readback; devices are not matched for speed comparison.

The [seed-stability audit](hung-development-seed-stability-20261029-v1/report.md) compares the 816 valid paired cells under identical station/day hashes. Of 72 method × reference × budget groups, 66 have all three seeds 7/11/19; six Seasonal Naive groups have seed 7 only and a null seed span. Four three-seed groups change the sign of their station-first MAE gain across seeds. Among 264 station × comparison strata with three seeds, 49 include both signs or zero. These strata share stations and references and are not an independent win rate.

The [saved-checkpoint context/proxy audit](hung-development-context-proxy-20261029-v2/report.md) independently reopens 782 new-job archives and the separate 69-run historical Main capsule. Its 851 runs contain 51,134 issued origins, of which 28,638 are scored (687,312 scored hours); the recomputed live MAE/RMSE match each saved manifest within `1e-4` kWh. The [context tables](hung-development-context-proxy-20261029-v2/context_error.csv) report original-kWh error by method, budget, weekday/weekend and local target hour with explicit scored-hour denominators. They are descriptive Development slices, with repeated stations/seeds and missing-label cells; they are not independent samples, a season-generalization result or a new method ranking. The [proxy table](hung-development-context-proxy-20261029-v2/proxy_decisions.csv) binds 1,242 Source Error, Uncertainty and Drift decisions to artifact hashes. Uncertainty chose retention 0 at all 414 decisions, so its signal–choice association is not estimable. Source Error and Drift show varying choices, but their signal scales differ and observed policy choices do not prove lower forecast error. The historical capsule has manifest/checkpoint bindings but no per-job receipts, unlike the 782 new jobs.

## Interpretation and method gate

The results make the Development accounting reproducible and explain the 407 null run scores within the frozen input. They do not answer effectiveness for those missing-label cells. The [Block7 availability audit](hung-block7-payload-availability-20261028.md) found no exact historical checkpoint file bytes in repository-local direct files or ZIP members; this does not refute a prior deserialized-payload-equality claim, but that claim still needs reviewer-verifiable bytes and numeric/per-origin tolerance. A [69-job contingency ledger](hung-block7-replay-contingency-20261029-v2/README.md) preflights new IDs and matched Main checkpoint hashes without submission. Run those jobs only if the reviewer explicitly rejects or requires replay, after a fresh code/config/jobset/capacity lock.

The [real Development parity trace](hung-q-real-development-input-trace-20261028-v1.md) gives the two runners the same 14 station-1001 origins and next-day labels. Their label-access schedule and normalized core event chronology match, while forecasts differ by up to 0.292965 normalized units and active GRU encoders after the block by 0.024327. Both keep lambda 0 in this diagnostic block, but lower bounds differ. The heads were randomly initialized and unmatched; this is neither trained-state parity nor a method choice. The [reviewer contract packet](hung-parity-contract-decision-packet-20261029-v1.md) separates what Report 1 fixes from open head/bootstrap conventions. A non-author reviewer must choose the correctness-based contract and require a matched trained-state, gradient/optimizer/resume trace with declared tolerance. No choice may be made for favorable Development or Final MAE.

RQ1–RQ3 therefore have bounded Development evidence only: observed paired errors and adverse transfer on the scorable subset, seed variation, context-sliced errors, controller/proxy actions, explicit missingness, and measured final-stage costs. A causal or generalizable proxy–error relationship, independent CHARGED geography, Block7 comparability, common implementation parity and Final generalization remain unproven.
