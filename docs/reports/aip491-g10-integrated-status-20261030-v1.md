## Metrics and interpretation

Hùng's [checkpoint-recomputed Final tables](https://github.com/HungLD9505/adaptive-retention-ev/tree/207255b9caf33c4221daab560bb60af6409d416f/docs/reports/hung-final-analysis-20261029-v1) give Main MAE **8.051 / 8.042 / 7.916 kWh** at 7/14/28 days. Fixed retention MAE is **7.961 / 7.952 / 7.860 kWh**, slightly lower than Main at every budget. Source-error and several other alternatives also have lower observed MAE. The [WAPE audit](https://github.com/HungLD9505/adaptive-retention-ev/tree/207255b9caf33c4221daab560bb60af6409d416f/docs/reports/hung-final-wape-20261029-v1) validates denominators for 360/360 jobs. These are bounded descriptive results, not proof of controller superiority or population generalization.

Qyroven's UrbanEV **Development** Adaptive MAE is **4.8420 / 5.0988 / 5.5244 kWh**, versus Fixed **4.8081 / 5.0570 / 5.4998 kWh**. The six CHARGED city Development results vary by city; the [handoff](https://github.com/HungLD9505/adaptive-retention-ev/blob/377723f5d55a91cde6229807300429365c764117/docs/reports/issue23-development-handoff.md) says every exploratory Adaptive-minus-Fixed interval crosses zero. Hùng Final and Qyroven Development MAEs must not be ranked across implementations because the entities, seeds, data roles and protocols differ. No test-set result is used here to choose a model, threshold or calibration.
