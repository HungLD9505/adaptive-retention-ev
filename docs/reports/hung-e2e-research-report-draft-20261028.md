## Abstract

We study 24-hour-ahead hourly energy forecasting for stations with 7, 14 or 28 days of initial target history. The proposed method transfers a GRU from pre-deployment source data, penalizes movement from a frozen source representation during target adaptation, and selects among three retention levels using arrived paired forecast errors. Hung's attributable UrbanEV/SZH Development execution reached 782/782 new jobs. Saved provider reports claim full remote archive readback, while the later local audit still flags shard0039's remote-version binding and reopens only 222 jobs' actual archive, receipt, manifest and member bytes; 560 Kaggle jobs await locally reviewable per-job proof. The latest authenticated portable metric snapshot has 371 physical runs, of which 196 are scorable and 175 have no complete-label scored day. These observations establish execution and a partial comparison catalog. They do not answer full-cohort effectiveness or Final generalization questions.

## Introduction and motivation

Charging load varies across hours and stations, while a newly deployed target may offer only a short usable history. The project's fixed task is to forecast the next 24 hourly energy values from the previous 24 hours at a daily origin, with permitted future calendar inputs. Transfer from older stations may reduce the data needed at a target, but a fixed constraint on source representations could also suppress useful adaptation. The proposed controller tests whether observed paired errors from several retention levels provide a better basis for changing that constraint than fixed retention or indirect source-error, uncertainty and drift proxies. This is the hypothesis from Report 1, not an empirical conclusion of the current draft.

## Research questions and claims under test

- **RQ1:** How does retention affect original-kWh forecast error, and when do source-error, uncertainty or drift proxies fail to reflect paired differences between retention levels?
- **RQ2:** Does the confidence controller improve on fixed retention, Online Best-lambda and proxy controllers under the same forecasts, labels, candidate update budget and station-level aggregation?
- **RQ3:** How stable are the results across stations, 7/14/28-day budgets, seeds and observed contexts, and what are the full candidate/audit compute and storage costs?

These are questions, not answered claims. A better MAE in one observed pair does not establish transfer benefit, novelty, statistical significance or controller superiority. The exact paired cohorts and missing cells must accompany every estimate.

## Data, forecasting task and causal protocol

UrbanEV/SZH is the primary observed cohort. Hourly station energy is estimated from the accepted data pipeline, not asserted to be a directly metered reading. A daily origin uses the last 24 hourly history values, observed/missing masks and permitted known future calendar to produce 24 nonnegative hourly forecasts. Source data are restricted to the pre-deployment partition. Target adaptation uses only the assigned initial history budget. Forecasts are stored before labels are read; a day scores and updates only after all 24 valid labels are available. Missing labels are neither filled nor scored as zero. The [input contract](../data/INPUT_ACCEPTANCE.md), [method report](hung-39-method-implementation.md) and [phase status](hung-provider-phase-results-20261028.md) define actual boundaries and identities.

The intended separate six-city CHARGED supplementary scope is reported descriptively in the [proof summary](hung-charged-supplementary-proof-summary-20261028.md). It contains 2,268 tracked proof rows, including adverse AMS rows, but remote executor attribution, relation to Qyroven's primary matrix, source/input lineage and independent daily-byte review are open. SZH may overlap UrbanEV. These rows cannot yet be called Hung's accepted independent cross-dataset E2E result.

## Method and executed configuration

Hung's executed backbone has a two-layer, hidden-64 GRU and 24-output calendar/Softplus head, 41,696 parameters. Source training used one pass over 65,888 eligible examples from 871 source IDs, 2,059 Adam updates per Source stage. A separate Target optimizer adapted an initialized model; a frozen evaluation-mode Source encoder provided latent reference and source-only scale. Forecast MAE on complete normalized targets plus lambda-weighted squared scaled latent distance formed the training objective. `lambda=0` means Source-initialized full fine-tuning, not Target-only training.

The prequential runner forks the same active model/optimizer into paired candidates at `lambda={0,0.1,0.2}`, with 14-day blocks, a five-complete-day minimum and one-sided two-challenger day-block-bootstrap lower-bound decision. The diagnostic implementation uses block length two and 100 resamples. The chosen trained candidate, including a kept active candidate, carries forward; an isolated old-policy audit does not influence decisions. [#39 method and runner evidence](hung-39-method-implementation.md) explains the exact code and resume limits. These settings need Development and peer review before any immutable Hung Final lock.

The required comparison protocol includes nine baseline families and five ablation categories across 69 logical UrbanEV/SZH station/budget/seed cells. Exact aliases reuse physical artifacts. The [frozen allocation](evidence/hung-development-allocation-20261027-v1.json) schedules 782 new jobs: pilot six, local CUDA 216 and Kaggle 560. Historical Main 69 and Block7 69 are separate from the new-job count. The latter remain unmatched pending the [fairness decision](hung-block7-fairness-20261027-v1.md).

## Discussion and limitations at this checkpoint

RQ1 cannot be answered across the required cohort until the missing method cells and exact paired label intersections are authenticated. Proxy outputs are estimator observations, not a direct measure of the value of latent retention. The present Target-only-reference rows are based on single observed stations and cannot provide a cohort negative-transfer estimate.

RQ2 remains open because fixed policies, Online Best-lambda, proxies and confidence are not all covered on the same verified station/seed/budget days. The 32 switches demonstrate that the controller path ran; they do not show that switching helped or reduced harm. The [parity audit](hung-q-parity-20261027-v1.md) found matching encoder/retention fixture tensors but different complete head topologies and a bootstrap convention that reverses a marginal keep/switch fixture. An independently reviewed contract and shared event trace are required before treating implementations as interchangeable.

RQ3 remains open for full station/time uncertainty, context thresholds, seed sensitivity and whole-method cost. Missing days and 175 zero-scored runs in the current metric snapshot limit inference. The CHARGED proof's adverse AMS rows and possible SZH overlap must remain visible; neither favorable nor adverse rows may be used to revise the predeclared Final design. No known intervention/event and locked recovery-time definition are present, so no recovery-time metric is invented.
